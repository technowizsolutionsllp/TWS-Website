# Website Copy Briefs

## Homepage

Primary headline:

> Technowiz Solutions builds products that make file decisions clearer.

Supporting copy:

> We design, build, license, and support practical software products that help people clean, compress, and organize local files with privacy, explanation, and control.

Primary CTA:

> View products

Secondary CTA:

> Start a conversation

Trust strip:

- Local privacy first: Sensitive documents and file libraries stay on the user machine, which makes trust clear before features are compared.
- Review before change: The products show what they found, what they recommend, and what should be reviewed before anything important changes.
- Enterprise-ready proof: Protected workflows, previews, recovery paths, audit logs, local licensing, and native integration support serious file work.

Services:

- Digital software product development.
- Application design and engineering.
- ITES and support services.
- Technology consulting and advisory.
- Product licensing and commercialization.
- Import, export, and distribution of software solutions.

## Product Page Formula

1. Start with the concrete customer outcome.
2. Name the common market pitch.
3. Explain the Technowiz advantage in simple language.
4. Show the workflow in three steps.
5. Add trust proof from the product architecture.
6. Identify the best initial audiences.
7. Close with launch, download, or contact CTA.

## NoDupe Product Page

Headline:

> Build one trusted library from messy backups without deleting the wrong copy.

Short pitch:

> NoDupe helps people turn copied folders, phone imports, old drives, and backups into one trusted file library.

Simple advantage:

> Safe consolidation, not duplicate detection alone.

Supporting proof:

- Protected Master/Target workflow.
- Exact, Similar, and Deep scan modes.
- Explainable keep recommendations.
- Recycle Bin default and optional VSS Guaranteed Undo.
- Verified Copy-to-Master before cleanup.
- Action logs and Business audit export.

Audience:

- Home users consolidating old drives and phone imports.
- Creators cleaning photo, video, and audio libraries.
- Small teams needing reviewable cleanup and audit exports.

Supporting proof:

- Smart Analyze document breakdown.

## Public Voice Rules

Use simple phrases:

- "Files stay local."
- "Preview before saving."
- "Review before cleanup."
- "Understand what changed."
- "Protected folders stay protected."

Avoid internal strategy words in public copy:

- moat
- defensibility
- commodity job
- wedge
- table stakes
